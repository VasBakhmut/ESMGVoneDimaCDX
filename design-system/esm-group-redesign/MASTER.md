# ESM Group design system

## Direction

Organic trade-service editorial: direct, approachable and evidence-led. The visual language combines commercial building photography, regulatory clarity, warm ivory space and large golden forms taken from ESM Group's existing identity.

## Tokens

- Cream surface: `#F7F4EC`
- Paper surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Charcoal navy: `#0B1D34` (text and small contrast surfaces only)
- ESM golden yellow: `#F3A712`
- Warm ivory canvas: `#FFFDF7`
- Pale gold surface: `#FFF7DF`
- Muted steel: `#606B6F`
- Display: Barlow Condensed, 600
- Body: Manrope, 400–700
- Corners: intentionally asymmetric 8–34px for controls and cards; organic capsule treatment for hero photography
- Shadows: restrained depth on service cards and photographic annotations

## Layout

- Desktop shell: 1320px maximum with 40px minimum gutters.
- Hero: asymmetric 46/54 split between copy and architecture photography.
- Homepage: strong hero followed by a restrained facts strip, complete services grid, sectors, process, resources and FAQs.
- Services: eight substantive cards in a 4×2 desktop grid, each linking to its own indexable service page and written around a distinct search intent.
- Process numbering is allowed because Review → Inspect → Report is a real sequence.

## Signature interaction

- The Compliance Navigator lets visitors choose one of four real building situations and immediately see a practical starting pathway.
- It supports discovery and internal linking; it never claims to replace review of the building's approved documents.
- Large golden rounded section transitions carry the reference's visual energy without reproducing its plumbing-specific artwork.

## Signature

The memorable element is the organic hero: a sharp Melbourne building photograph in a large asymmetric capsule set into a custom golden-yellow raster field.

## Interaction

- One golden-yellow primary action per view.
- Hover and focus states use color or surface changes without layout shift.
- Minimum 44px interactive targets.
- Reduced-motion preference is respected.
- Mobile prioritises the building image, headline and quote action; annotations are removed when space becomes tight.

## Do not use

- Statistics without verified source data
- Repeated stat strips or unverified counters
- Excessively pill-shaped or generic SaaS cards
- Glassmorphism, gradients or floating decorative blobs
- Fake clients, ratings, certifications or awards
- Decorative motion unrelated to an interaction
